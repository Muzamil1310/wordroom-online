Why this is different

One buffer, every tool

Competing sites make you paste your text again on every page. Here the text follows you: count it, clean it, convert it, score it, without ever re-pasting. Press ⌘K and run any transform on the text you already have.

Privacy is the product, not a policy page

Every calculation happens in your browser. There is no endpoint that receives your text, which means there is nothing to leak, nothing to log and no file-size limit. Open your network tab and watch it stay quiet while you type.

Correct, not approximately correct

Word counts follow Unicode segmentation rules, so Telugu, Hindi and Chinese count properly instead of being split on spaces. Title case follows actual AP and Chicago rules instead of capitalising every word. Academic mode excludes what your marker excludes.

Built for the keyboard

Every action has a keyboard route. ⌘K opens the palette, / focuses search, ⌘⇧F enters focus mode. No modal you cannot dismiss, no interstitial before a result.
